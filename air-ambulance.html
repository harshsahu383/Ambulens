    When Do You Need Air Ambulance?

    An air ambulance is required for rapid, specialized transport of critically ill or injured patients when ground transport is too slow, unavailable, or unable to provide necessary life support.

    • Critical patient transfer between cities
    • Emergency evacuation
    • Organ transplant logistics
    • Long-distance patient transportation
    • International medical repatriation
    • Patients requiring continuous ICU care
